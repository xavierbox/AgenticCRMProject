### Explanation of Efficiency Calculation:
Efficiency is computed as the ratio of the total oil production attributed to an injector to the total water injected by that injector. The attributable oil production is determined by summing the contributions from all producers connected to the injector. For each producer, the contribution is calculated as:

\[ \text{Attributable Oil} = \text{GAIN} \times \text{Producer's Liquid Production} \times \text{Oil Fraction} \]

The total water injected is derived from the sum of the product of GAIN and the producer's liquid production for all connected producers. The efficiency metric provides insight into how effectively an injector contributes to oil production relative to the amount of water it injects.
